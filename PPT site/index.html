The True Cost to Planet & People

💧

Water Use & Pollution

  • ➤2,000 gallons of water for one pair of jeans.
  • ➤Textile dyeing is the #2 industrial water polluter globally.
  • ➤500,000 tons of microfibers enter oceans annually.
🌍

Carbon Footprint & Waste

  • ➤Contributes ~10% of global CO₂ emissions—more than flights & shipping.
  • ➤85% of all textiles are landfilled or incinerated yearly.
  • ➤Only ~8% of textile fibers are actually recycled.
🧵

Social Impact & Labor

  • ➤Fewer than 2% of 75M garment workers earn a living wage.
  • ➤Unsafe conditions led to tragedies like the Rana Plaza collapse.
  • ➤Cases of child labor and unpaid wages persist in supply chains.

Stitching a Better Future

Circular Economy Initiatives

Brands like Patagonia are proving resale is profitable. Industry-wide projects like the "Jeans Redesign" are creating clothing that's easier to recycle from the start.

🇮🇳 India's Circular Potential

India generates ~7.7 million tonnes of textile waste annually. By formalizing sorting, 1.38 million tonnes could be reclaimed, generating up to ₹1,348 crore.

Material Innovations

New materials like Mycelium leather (from fungi), Piñatex (from pineapples), and recycled fibers are cutting resource use and offering sustainable alternatives.

Your Voice, Your Power

A new generation is demanding change. Through powerful campaigns and student-led action on campus, we are shifting the narrative. By promoting reuse, demanding transparency, and choosing consciously, we can collectively rewrite the future of fashion.
